# Curriculum alignment — Norwegian upper-secondary biology

**Status:** v0.1 · 26 October 2026  
**Owner:** Snorre (design) · Henrik and Hanna (theory review) · Johannes and Marie (GDD review)  
**Primary curriculum:** Norwegian upper-secondary biology, **Biologi 1 (BIO01-02), VG2**  
**Secondary use:** VG3 revision and first-year anatomy & physiology bridge — **not** a formal VG3 curriculum claim  
**Source:** Udir, *Læreplan i biologi (BIO01-02)*, competence aims after Biologi 1, <https://www.udir.no/lk20/bio01-02/kompetansemaal-og-vurdering/kv538?lang=nob>, accessed 26 October 2026.

> The game is a **supplement**, not a full curriculum. It covers a slice of 2–3 competence aims through the knee-jerk reflex arc. The alignment table says exactly what is covered, partially covered, and not covered.

---

## 1. Why Biologi 1, not “VG2/VG3 biology”

The nervous system, reflex arc, and synapse are **Biologi 1 (VG2)** content. Biologi 2 (VG3) focuses on populations, ecology, energy flow, enzymes, genetics, evolution, and biotechnology. VG3 students can use the game for revision or transfer, but claiming a formal VG3 alignment would be inaccurate.

**Decision:** primary alignment is Biologi 1 (VG2). VG3 and first-year A&P are secondary/revision audiences.

## 2. Exact competence aims

Quoted from Udir (Bokmål). English translations are working translations for the design team, not official translations.

| ID | Udir competence aim (Bokmål) | Working English translation | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | “utforske sammenhenger mellom anatomi og fysiologi og gjøre rede for prinsippene for livsprosessene i organismer” | Explore relationships between anatomy and physiology and explain the principles of life processes in organisms. | **Primary.** The reflex arc is a structure–function/physiology example. |
| **A2** | “utforske sammenhenger mellom cellestrukturer og -funksjoner og gjøre rede for hvordan cellulære membraner danner grunnlag for kommunikasjon mellom celler” | Explore relationships between cell structures and functions and explain how cellular membranes form the basis for communication between cells. | **Partial.** The synapse/neurotransmitter is cell communication, but the game does not cover membranes in depth. |
| **A3** | “gjøre rede for hvordan utvalgte reguleringsmekanismer styrer homeostase hos mennesket, og undersøke hvordan livsstil kan påvirke disse mekanismene” | Explain how selected regulatory mechanisms control homeostasis in humans and investigate how lifestyle can affect these mechanisms. | **Partial/stretch.** The reflex is a regulatory mechanism, but the game does not cover homeostasis broadly or lifestyle. |
| **A4** | “planlegge og gjennomføre undersøkelser, samle, behandle og tolke data og presentere resultater og funn” | Plan and carry out investigations, collect, process and interpret data, and present results and findings. | **Not covered.** The game is not a laboratory investigation. |

**Do not claim full coverage of A1–A4.** The GDD/theory should say “aligned to a slice of A1 and A2, with a partial link to A3.”

## 4. Core vs advanced depth

The cards use OpenStax *A&P* (first-year college). For VG2, the game uses only the **core** facts. Advanced facts stay optional (scan/debrief/first-year bridge).

| Card | Core for VG2 | Advanced / optional |
|---|---|---|
| **NERV-01** Neuron | Facts 1, 3, 5, 6: neuron parts/one-way; myelin; threshold/action potential; all-or-none/refractory | Facts 2, 4: axon hillock/initial segment; resting potential/Na⁺–K⁺ pump |
| **NERV-02** Synapse | Facts 1, 2, 5: synapse parts; Ca²⁺-triggered release; neuromuscular junction/ACh | Facts 3, 4, 6: EPSP/IPSP; summation; clearance/electrical synapses |
| **NERV-03** Reflex | Facts 1, 2, 3, 5: muscle spindle; dorsal root; ventral root; brain later | Fact 4: reciprocal inhibition (optional) |

**Rule:** VG2 gates and debrief prompts use core facts only. Advanced facts can appear in optional scan targets or a “deeper look” debrief, but they are not required for completion.

**Claim:** the game supports explanation and prediction in a short session. **Non-claim:** it does not replace the Biologi 1 course, laboratory work, or teacher instruction; it does not improve exam scores (not tested).

---

## 6. What the game does not cover

- The full nervous system (brain anatomy, autonomic nervous system, pain pathways).
- The full skeletal/muscular systems (only the bone/joint/effector context needed for the reflex arc).
- Homeostasis broadly (A3 is partial).
- Laboratory investigation/data handling (A4).
- Disease, diagnosis, or clinical training.
- The full Biologi 1 or Biologi 2 curriculum.

---

## 7. VG3 and first-year use

- **VG3 revision:** the game can be used as a quick refresher before genetics/ecology work; no formal VG3 aim is claimed.
- **First-year A&P bridge:** advanced facts (EPSP/IPSP, summation, resting potential) are optional; first-year students can use them without changing the VG2 core.
