3. Fonts

Silkscreen and Pixelify Sans are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts are copied next to the font files:

final/shared/fonts/
  OFL-Silkscreen.txt
  OFL-PixelifySans.txt
  NOTICES.md

Keep the OFL text with any redistribution of the font files. The GDD itself does not need to embed the font file; it only needs the attribution/licence note if the font is redistributed.

4. Gaps to resolve before submission

  1. Ludo/Nano Banana plan and terms URL. The current labels say “plan TBD”. Record the actual plan and terms URL, or keep the POC wording and state that the assets are prototype-only.
  2. Prompt/model/seed log. The M3 prompt pack has a log template but no filled rows. Add a row per accepted asset (tool, prompt version, model, seed, date, plan/terms URL, chosen file).
  3. Anatomy licenses.json completeness. Add the new entity IDs if they are going to be described as final. The current POC build can keep the placeholder wording.
  4. Hub licences file. Consider adding public/hub/bathroom/licenses.json for parity with the packs, or keep the embedded strings and say so in the report.
  5. Raw hub source batch. The ignored new-main-menu/ batch is absent; the processed exports/reports are the only record. Do not claim the hub is fully reproducible from source.
  6. Final approval. POC-integrated sprites and the pretty sky are not final approved. The GDD/video should say “POC integrated; final approval pending” where that distinction matters.
  7. Placeholders and final art. The badge, scan.neuron, scan.pathway, scan.reflex, the Silicon interrupt/exit, the 03/04 far/mid/near layers, the Silicon circuit atlas, the hub logo CSS fallback, and the remaining audio are placeholders. The 2026-11-03 04 batch (sky, four atlases, gate, exit) is final and licensed. Do not present placeholders or raw source JPEGs as final runtime assets.

5. Suggested report wording

The visual assets are AI-generated in Ludo.ai / Nanobanan (Google Gemini) under the group’s art direction, then keyed, fitted, palette-quantised and packed locally. The current repository labels them as POC assets with the final plan/terms still TBD. The two typefaces are Silkscreen and Pixelify Sans, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The in-repo placeholder atlases are CC0-1.0. No third-party images or audio are included.

Do not write “all assets are original” without also saying that the art is AI-generated and that the final commercial plan is unresolved.
