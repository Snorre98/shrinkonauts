GDD map — what to use where

Copied from the working docs. Some cross-links may still point to the original repository or Obsidian vault.

This is the bridge between the asset library and the GBL GDD. It follows the course template: Learning, Gameplay, UI/Controls and Monetization are mandatory; Story, Characters, Level/Environment, Art, Sound & Music and Accessibility are optional.

Read this first: the committed gameplay screenshots in this pack are placeholder-era. Use the gameplay walkthrough for current captures: walkthrough.html. It covers hub, launch, 03, 04, Silicon, solo/co-op, text scale, reduced motion and key art, with captions and metadata. The old screenshots remain the development record.

Online: the gallery is at https://snorre98.github.io/shrinkonauts/gdd/; the walkthrough is at /gdd/walkthrough.html. Paths beginning contact-sheets/ or gdd-picks/ refer to the local pack; the web gallery replaces those with searchable groups.

Sound & Music

Three pilot SFX exist (scan, jump, land); the other 15 cues and all four themes are placeholders. Cue playback and the ADR-0077 music bus/theme player are implemented, but the four charted themes are proposed and no theme files exist, so music is silent. The cue vocabulary is in final/anatomy/metadata/theme.json and final/hub/metadata/theme.json. Use those lists to write the cue table, and state clearly that the prototype has no shipped music or ambience; the theme player exists but resolves to silence until files land.

Accessibility

The shipped accessibility features are keyboard-only input, text scale, reduced motion, mute, pause, and no audio-only cues. Current captures: hub-settings, text-scale-125 and reduced-motion. The latter two show the settings in context; use them with the written feature list.
